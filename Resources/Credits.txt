KITNAP - credits and licences
=============================

KITNAP is made by FortyVolts. It uses the work below; each is used under the
licence named with it, and the full texts of those licences follow.


AI models
---------

Drum separation (the drums out of the song)
  BS-RoFormer, 4 stems, trained and released by Roman Solovyev (ZFTurbo) in
  Music-Source-Separation-Training
  (https://github.com/ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training).
  MIT License, Copyright (c) 2024 Roman Solovyev (ZFTurbo).
  The network code is BS-RoFormer by Phil Wang
  (https://github.com/lucidrains/BS-RoFormer), MIT License,
  Copyright (c) 2023 Phil Wang.

Drum split (the drums into kick, snare, hi-hat and cymbals)
  KITNAP's own model, trained by FortyVolts. Its network (MDX23C) follows the
  one in Music-Source-Separation-Training by Roman Solovyev (ZFTurbo), MIT
  License, Copyright (c) 2024 Roman Solovyev (ZFTurbo).
  It was trained on examples made from, among others:

  - Groove MIDI Dataset, by Jon Gillick, Adam Roberts, Jesse Engel, Douglas
    Eck and David Bamman, released by Google LLC under the Creative Commons
    Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).
    https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove
    J. Gillick, A. Roberts, J. Engel, D. Eck and D. Bamman, "Learning to
    Groove with Inverse Sequence Transformations", ICML 2019.

  - StemGMD, by Alessandro Ilic Mezza, Riccardo Giampiccolo, Alberto
    Bernardini and Augusto Sarti (Politecnico di Milano), released under the
    Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).
    https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7860223
    A. I. Mezza, R. Giampiccolo, A. Bernardini and A. Sarti, "Toward Deep
    Drum Source Separation", Pattern Recognition Letters, vol. 183,
    pp. 86-91, 2024.

  - Drum sounds from Freesound (https://freesound.org) that their makers
    released into the public domain (CC0).

  The datasets were cut up, mixed and processed into training examples; none
  of them is included in KITNAP. CC BY 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
